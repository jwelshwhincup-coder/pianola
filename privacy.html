Privacy Policy

Last updated 22 November 2026

The short version
Pianola has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Your progressions and preferences stay on your device and in your own iCloud. The only data that leaves your device is what Apple and our payments provider need to handle a subscription.

Who we are

Pianola is made by James Welsh-Whincup, an independent developer based in the United Kingdom, who is the data controller for the app. Contact: jwelshwhincup@gmail.com.

Data stored on your device

  • Your saved progressions and their names, your recently played list, and your settings.
  • Your taste profile: the likes, dislikes and choices Pianola uses to suggest progressions you'll enjoy.

This data stays on your device. We can't see it and it isn't sent to us.

iCloud

If you're signed in to iCloud, your saved progressions sync between your devices using Apple's CloudKit. They're stored in your private iCloud database, which only you can access. We can't see this data. Apple's handling of it is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Subscriptions and purchases

Payments are processed by Apple. We never see your name, email address or payment details.

We use RevenueCat to check whether you have Pianola Pro. To do that, RevenueCat receives:

  • A random, anonymous ID created by the app (not your Apple Account).
  • Your purchase and subscription history for Pianola (product, price, dates, trial status).
  • Basic device and app information, such as iOS version, app version, country and IP address, used to process the request.

We use this only to unlock Pro, restore purchases and understand how many people subscribe. It is never used for advertising or sold to anyone. The legal basis is performing our contract with you (providing what you paid for) and our legitimate interest in running the service.

Notifications

Pianola can send two kinds of reminder, both scheduled on your device:

  • If you start a free trial, a reminder two days before it ends.
  • If you're shown a limited-time offer, a reminder a few hours before it ends.

They're only sent if you allow notifications, never between 9 pm and 9 am, and no data is sent to us. You can turn them off any time in Settings → Notifications → Pianola.

What we don't do

  • No advertising, and no ad or tracking SDKs.
  • No analytics or crash-reporting services.
  • No tracking across other apps or websites.
  • No selling or sharing of personal data.

How long data is kept

On-device data is kept until you delete it or delete the app. iCloud data is kept until you delete it from iCloud. RevenueCat keeps purchase records for as long as needed to provide your subscription and meet legal and accounting requirements.

Your rights

Under UK and EU data protection law you can ask to access, correct or delete your personal data, or object to how it's used. To delete everything:

  • Delete the app to remove on-device data.
  • Remove Pianola's iCloud data in Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Storage → Pianola.
  • Email us to have your purchase records deleted from RevenueCat.

You can also complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Children

Pianola is suitable for all ages and doesn't knowingly collect personal data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.

Restore Pianola Pro

Open Settings → Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account you subscribed with. This works on all your iPhones and iPads.

Cancel or change your subscription

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. In Pianola go to Settings → Manage Subscription, or on your iPhone open Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions → Pianola. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date to avoid being charged. Cancelling during the free trial means you won't be charged.

Refunds

Payments are handled by Apple, so refunds are too. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

My saves aren't syncing

Saves sync through iCloud. Make sure you're signed in to the same Apple Account on each device, and that iCloud is on for Pianola in Settings → [your name] → iCloud. Syncing can take a minute.

Reset my taste

Pianola learns which progressions you like. To start fresh, go to Settings → Reset My Taste. Your saves are kept.

No sound

Check that your iPhone isn't on silent mode and that the volume is up. If you use Bluetooth headphones, try disconnecting and reconnecting them.
